# 🎯 Lakshya - Brand Identity

## Name & Meaning

**Lakshya** (लक्ष्य)
- **Sanskrit/Hindi**: Target, Goal, Aim, Objective
- **Pronunciation**: LUKSH-yuh
- **Cultural Significance**: Deep-rooted in Indian culture, instantly recognizable

## Tagline

**"Your Goal, Your Path, Your Success"**

Alternative taglines:
- "अपना लक्ष्य, अपना रास्ता" (Apna Lakshya, Apna Raasta)
- "Transform Goals into Reality"
- "Every Goal Has a Path"

## Brand Values

1. **Clarity** - Making complex goals simple and achievable
2. **Empowerment** - Students take control of their journey
3. **Intelligence** - AI-powered personalization
4. **Cultural Relevance** - Built for Indian students
5. **Achievement** - Focus on results and success

## Target Audience

**Primary**: Indian college students (18-25 years)
- GATE aspirants
- Placement seekers
- Startup founders
- Higher studies applicants
- Skill learners

**Secondary**: Self-learners, professionals, anyone with goals

## Voice & Tone

- **Motivational** but not preachy
- **Intelligent** but not complex
- **Supportive** like a mentor
- **Cultural** with Hindi/English mix where appropriate
- **Action-oriented** - focus on "doing"

## Visual Identity

### Colors (Suggested)
- **Primary**: Deep Orange/Saffron (#FF6B35) - Energy, ambition, Indian heritage
- **Secondary**: Navy Blue (#1E3A8A) - Trust, intelligence, stability
- **Accent**: Gold (#F59E0B) - Achievement, success
- **Neutral**: Slate Gray for backgrounds

### Logo Concepts
- Target/bullseye icon (literal lakshya)
- Arrow hitting target
- Mountain peak with flag
- Path/roadmap visualization
- Abstract "ल" character stylization

### Typography
- **Headings**: Bold, modern sans-serif (Inter, Poppins)
- **Body**: Clean, readable (Inter, Open Sans)
- **Accent**: Consider Devanagari script for "लक्ष्य" in logo
